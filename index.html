Underwriting Workbench Modernization

▼

Business Problem

Legacy monolithic underwriting system with 20+ year old codebase caused slow time-to-market, scalability bottlenecks, and limited innovation capabilities for new insurance products.

AI Policy Document Generation

▼

Business Problem

Manual policy document creation took 2-3 hours per document with 15% error rate. Compliance team struggled with consistency across 500+ document types.

Constraints

  • Must maintain regulatory compliance and audit trail
  • Support for complex legal language and state-specific requirements
  • Integration with existing document management system

Key Decisions

  • LLM-powered generation using GPT-4 with custom fine-tuning
  • RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation) architecture with vector database (Pinecone)
  • Human-in-the-loop validation workflow

Trade-offs

  • AI unpredictability vs. efficiency gains (mitigated with validation layer)
  • Cost of LLM API calls vs. labor savings

Business Outcome

✅ Reduced document generation time by 85% (15 minutes average) | Error rate decreased to <2% | $2M annual cost savings | 10,000+ documents generated

Architecture Decision Records

Key architectural decisions and the reasoning behind them:

Why Domain-Driven Design?

DDD provides a shared language between business and engineering, reducing misunderstandings and enabling faster iteration. By modeling bounded contexts around business domains, we achieved better team autonomy and reduced coupling between services. Critical for complex insurance domain with 50+ business concepts.

Why Event-Driven Architecture?

Event-driven patterns enable loose coupling and temporal decoupling between services, critical for scaling independent teams. Events provide natural audit trail for regulatory compliance. Chosen over synchronous APIs for better resilience - if a downstream service fails, events are persisted and can be replayed. Trade-off: Eventually consistent vs. immediate consistency.

Why Snowflake?

Snowflake's separation of compute and storage eliminates the traditional data warehouse bottleneck. Zero-copy cloning enables isolated dev/test environments without data duplication costs. Native JSON support and semi-structured data handling perfect for our polyglot data sources. 10x performance improvement over previous on-premise solution at 20% lower total cost.

Why Apache Kafka?

Kafka provides durability guarantees (replicated log) that message queues lack. Enables true pub-sub with multiple consumer groups reading same events independently. Consumer can replay events from any point in time - essential for debugging production issues and migrating to new services. Handles 10M+ events/day with <100ms latency.

Why Microservices?

Microservices enable independent deployment cycles - critical when coordinating 5+ engineering teams. Technology heterogeneity allows choosing the right tool per service (Python for ML, Java for high-throughput APIs). Failure isolation prevents cascading failures. Trade-off: Operational complexity (monitoring, distributed tracing) vs. team autonomy and deployment flexibility. Worth it at our scale (15+ services, 40+ engineers).
