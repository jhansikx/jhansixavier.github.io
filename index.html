Background

About Me

I'm a PhD student at Florida Atlantic University, advised by Dr. KwangSoo Yang. My research sits at the intersection of machine learning, graph theory, and spatial computing — where formal guarantees matter as much as empirical performance.

My dissertation introduces the Constraint-First design paradigm: instead of learning unconstrained outputs and fixing violations after the fact, structural constraints are enforced by construction. This approach eliminates entire classes of errors before they can occur.

I apply this paradigm to synthetic transportation network generation — producing planar, connected, degree-bounded road graphs whose geometry matches real OpenStreetMap data with sub-meter Fréchet distance.

Prior to my PhD, I completed a data science specialization on Coursera and have broad experience with statistical modeling, geospatial analysis, and deep learning pipelines.

HybridSTNG

Hybrid Framework for Synthetic Transportation Network Generation. The first method to guarantee planarity, connectivity, and degree bounds by construction while learning realistic road curvature via a GNN-VAE. Achieves 100% structural validity and a Fréchet distance of 0.48 m across all baseline comparisons.
